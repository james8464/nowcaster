# Historical GBP conversion sensitivity — 8 October 2026

Using timestamp-aligned OANDA practice historical-base EUR/GBP and GBP/USD bid/ask candles, the 32 previously tested product/rule/direction combinations were replayed once with time-varying GBP conversion. **None was positive in all three periods. No rule was promoted.** The complete 96 stage results are retained in [the companion CSV](intraday-fx-sensitivity-2026-10-08.csv); these are hypothetical GBP P&L totals, not a compounded account or executable returns.

The retained FX files are outside the repository at `/Users/james/Library/Application Support/Nowcaster/IntradayHistorical/fx-conversion-20261008/`. `EUR_GBP.jsonl` has 37,728 bars (SHA-256 `891c4365b67116dcd3d56896527543fd5be5ff009c40d684f2edd55004fab894`); `GBP_USD.jsonl` has 37,720 (SHA-256 `ffd7dee6417c725998fa86b02290c047557001bccdb6dc88785303156fb783cd`). Both cover 1 April through 30 September 2026, five-minute bars. Their source is historical broker *base* prices, not account-specific conversion factors or executable account quotes. Source-market JSONL identities and hashes are in `round-20261008/FEASIBILITY.md` beside the retained market files.

Timestamp overlap was 30,335/30,335 DE30, 35,871/35,871 SPX500, 37,719/37,727 EUR/USD and 35,721/35,721 WTICO source bars. All eight missing EUR/USD matches occurred after 21:00 UTC, outside the provisional 07:00–20:00 test window. Every replayed complete session had an exact FX bar at every source-bar timestamp; a missing rate now fails a session closed rather than carrying the prior rate forward.

The sensitivity kept the prior fixed periods and provisional windows: April–May development, June–July validation, August–September **already-inspected** historical period; 07:00–20:00 UTC for DE30/EUR_USD and 13:00–20:00 UTC for SPX500/WTICO. Complete sessions were 40/45/43, 41/43/42, 43/45/43 and 41/43/42 respectively. It used the same rule implementations, next-bar entry, adverse bid/ask exit, stop-first dual-touch resolution, £10,000 independent paper equity per day, 0.25% risk cap, and at most one trade per product/session. EUR gains convert at EUR/GBP bid and losses at ask; USD gains convert at the inverse GBP/USD ask and losses at the inverse bid. Entry sizing uses the loss side. An additional 1% conversion charge was retained for comparability; slippage, commission and financing remained zero. [OANDA UK currently publishes a 1% v20 conversion markup on the mid-rate](https://www.oanda.com/uk-en/trading/our-charges/), so charging 1% *on top of* the observed FX bid/ask here is a conservative sensitivity assumption, **not** an exact reconstruction of its account factor. These favorable and unverified execution-cost assumptions, missing exact holiday/DST calendars, and account-price differences mean the result is exploratory, not a qualification test. OANDA distinguishes [account gain, loss and position-value conversion factors](https://developer.oanda.com/rest-live-v20/pricing-df/) from historical FX candles.

All 96 cell signs matched the earlier fixed-rate sensitivity screen; small GBP differences did not repair a failed rule. August–September has already been inspected and may **not** be called sealed confirmation for any revised rule. Next work must be a separately registered hypothesis with verified product terms and future untouched/prospective data, while the live monitor stays diagnostic and the frozen BTC/ETH study stays unchanged.

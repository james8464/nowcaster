# SPX500 last-half-hour hypothesis: development failed

This is a separate, exploratory OANDA practice historical-CFD test, not a live account-quote result or advice. The [rule and split](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-08-spx-last-half-hour-research.md) were registered before returns were inspected. Code was committed at `53f353b`, corrected for regular-session slicing at `8707938`, and bound to its source hash at `8c57292` before the first corrected development run. None of the later corrections changed the trading formula, stop, target or cost assumption.

## Retained attempts and outcome

The first attempt, at `/Users/james/Library/Application Support/Nowcaster/LastHalfHourResearch/round-001/results/development.json`, produced **zero eligible days** because the runner mistakenly passed all overnight CFD candles to a 78-bar US-regular-session checker. This is a data-handling failure, not a P&L observation; its output is retained, not replaced. A regression test now verifies that overnight candles are excluded. The second attempt is at `/Users/james/Library/Application Support/Nowcaster/LastHalfHourResearch/round-002/results/development.json`.

The corrected 2022–2023 development sample has 481 complete regular sessions, 34 sessions with insufficient coverage, and 251 modeled trades (145 long, 106 short). There were 103 winning, 147 losing and one flat modeled trade; 230 eligible days abstained. At one CFD unit per trade, the fixed rule returned **−332.80426 USD index-point equivalents** after historical bid/ask spread, 0.5 index points adverse slippage on each side, and the declared 1% conversion-fee approximation. With 1.0 point adverse slippage per side it returned **−611.0202925**. The fixed-rule maximum peak-to-trough drawdown was 369.92826 point equivalents; the 95% daily bootstrap lower bound of mean points/day was −1.2670. Cash/no trade was 0, and always-long and always-short over the same last-25-minute eligible windows were −566.991 and −798.096 respectively. These figures are not GBP account equity, percentages or executable broker fills.

The rule fails in development; it is not selected. The 2024 validation and 2025 sealed prices were captured but **their returns have not been inspected** for this rule. This preserves those windows rather than using them to rescue a losing development result. All four historical captures remain outside the active study and were never used to warm live account-quote decisions.

## Independent review and calendar-corrected attempt

An independent review found that the second attempt's `incomplete_days` did not count wholly absent sessions and that a CFD can print a full 78-bar day when the US cash market is closed. This made the stage-coverage claim too permissive. The second output above is retained, not rewritten. Commit `957c4ed` adds a fixed 2022–2025 US cash-market calendar, excludes published holidays and early closes, counts every expected full session, checks the exact capture identity and requested range, records rejected attempts, and refuses to read a sealed capture until earlier gates pass. The calendar follows [ICE/NYSE's 2022–2024 schedule](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/nyse-group-announces-2022-2023-and-2024-holiday-and-early-closings-calendar-2021-12), [its 2025 schedule](https://s2.q4cdn.com/154085107/files/doc_news/NYSE-Group-Announces-2025-2026-and-2027-Holiday-and-Early-Closings-Calendar-2024.pdf), and [the January 9, 2025 special closure](https://www.nyse.com/publicdocs/nyse/markets/american-options/rule-interpretations/2025/National_Day_of_Mourning_20250102.pdf). These are SPY-style cash-session filters, not OANDA CFD trading-hour guarantees.

The third immutable development output is `/Users/james/Library/Application Support/Nowcaster/LastHalfHourResearch/round-003/results/development.json`. It counted **498 expected full sessions, 495 complete and three incomplete**: 99.3976% coverage, below the predeclared 99.5% minimum. It made 257 modeled trades (146 long, 111 short), with 106 wins, 150 losses, one flat and 238 no-trade decisions. The fixed rule lost **337.7973925 USD index-point equivalents per one CFD unit** after modeled costs; the stressed result lost 621.5071625. Long and short contributed −223.34473 and −114.4526625 respectively. Its mean daily result's 95% bootstrap interval ran from **−1.22068 to −0.12937 points/day**. Both the quality and P&L gates fail. No 2024 or 2025 outcome was opened by the third attempt, and the running account-quote study was not changed.

## Data identity and limitations

The retained 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025 M5 bid/ask JSONL captures contain respectively 70,642, 70,578, 70,858 and 70,512 bars. Their SHA-256 hashes are `f65f6b5440ba8d1d5ba5674cfb3b92dc59e241f4844987aba8aec4b59fb10be3`, `f9e0957a72124b3cb2feb7e9129eb9328612e3f8555e71784475fa7bae096bc6`, `4e671b718ea9772b27fc21caf41d95c47181b46e7e3cae10c9e00e437472ffad`, and `656140bb14c47c0915f3e854e06f29929201354572473e24f7e63f3e98952982`. The development combined-input hash and implementation hash are stored in each immutable result and protocol. The second attempt could not infer wholly absent market days from bars alone; it excluded 34 observed incomplete days without filling them. The third attempt instead uses the fixed cash-session calendar and counts missing full sessions. Historical OANDA base candles may differ from account-specific pricing; historical GBP conversion and financing were unavailable. Those omissions cannot be treated as zero real-world cost or a reason to promote the rule.

The motivating [SPY research](https://smallake.kr/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/SSRN-id2440866.pdf) found a statistical intraday relationship in a different instrument and era. This CFD test shows that its fixed, risk-capped translation is not profitable on this development sample after modeled costs. The active prospective account-quote study remains unchanged. No historical result can satisfy the separate 90-day/100-trade prospective gate or make a signal safe to copy with money.

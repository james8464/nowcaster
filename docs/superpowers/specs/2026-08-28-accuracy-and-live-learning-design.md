# Accuracy and Live Learning Reliability Design

**Date:** 2026-08-28  
**Status:** Approved  
**Scope:** Point-in-time market data, outcome-aligned prediction, execution-aware validation, governed self-improvement, live drift controls, and evidence-first macOS presentation

## Purpose

Nowcaster will estimate whether a hypothetical trade has positive net value under the exact venue, feed, horizon, liquidity, and regime represented by its evidence. It will optimize calibrated target-before-stop probability and conservative expected net return, not raw directional hit rate. It will abstain whenever data, calibration, execution, drift, or forward-evidence gates are not satisfied.

This design cannot make future profitability certain. It makes every claim measurable, falsifiable, versioned, and fail-closed. Paid data, exchange entitlements, broker permissions, and elapsed forward observation cannot be generated by software; the app exposes exact readiness gaps until those inputs exist.

## Invariants

- Only finalized observations available by decision time may influence a decision.
- Provider, feed, venue, instrument type, symbol, and timestamp semantics never splice silently.
- Spot, margin, perpetual, and composite instruments are distinct.
- Costs include observed spread plus configured fees, slippage, funding, borrow, latency, and impact buffers.
- A learning run cannot inspect sealed test outcomes or modify the live champion.
- A new champion creates a new immutable cohort and starts forward evidence from zero.
- Synthetic paths are stress tests, never proof of profitability.
- Live monitoring keeps a reserved compute budget and preempts research under pressure.
- No new path submits, replaces, or cancels broker orders.

## Architecture

### 1. Rich market-event contract

Historical bars retain quote-asset volume and taker-buy volume when supplied. Live quotes retain bid size, ask size, provider sequence, provider event time, receive time, and processing time. Normalized trade and depth-event types capture aggressor direction, price, size, depth level, update identifiers, and sequence continuity.

Raw events remain immutable. Derived bars and microstructure features reference the raw-event identity used to create them. Historical and live aggregation share one deterministic implementation. Incomplete event streams produce a scoped unavailable interval rather than a fabricated observation.

Equity subscriptions include trades, quotes, bars, trading status, LULD events, corrections, and cancel/error events when the selected feed supports them. Crypto subscriptions include aggregate trades, book ticker, depth deltas, and finalized klines. Provider decoders reject malformed, crossed, non-finite, out-of-order, or unreasonably future-dated events.

### 2. Instrument and data identity

The bundled crypto instruments are identified as Binance USDT spot instruments. A point-in-time instrument descriptor records venue, feed, quote currency, product type, short mechanism, funding applicability, borrow applicability, and trading calendar. A signal is ineligible when its requested direction cannot be implemented by that product.

Data-quality gates report completeness, duplicates, revisions, continuity, correction handling, event-time latency, and historical/live semantic parity per scope. Gaps are never filled. Research may evaluate authenticated contiguous segments when the protocol explicitly permits segment-level evidence; missing sections remain visible and cannot be treated as zero-return time.

### 3. Outcome-aligned labels

For every decision timestamp, a causal barrier label records:

- long and short target-before-stop outcome;
- net return after the execution model;
- maximum favourable excursion;
- maximum adverse excursion;
- first-passage time and exit reason; and
- the timestamp at which the full outcome became knowable.

If stop and target are both crossed inside a bar and event ordering is unavailable, the adverse outcome wins. Labels may use only future observations after the decision timestamp, and they are withheld from training until `outcome_available_at`.

The live planner uses empirical excursion and first-passage distributions from development data to select supported stop and target levels. It abstains when no level has positive conservative expected value after costs.

### 4. Calibration and selective prediction

Calibration is fit only from out-of-fold development predictions. The contract stores method, sample size, positive count, Brier score, log loss, expected calibration error, effective sample size, calibration slice, and confidence bounds. The minimum promotable calibration sample is 100 effective observations; isotonic calibration requires 1,000 unless a stricter configured threshold applies.

The final ensemble consumes out-of-fold component predictions. It penalizes redundant components, conditions on asset, product, direction, horizon, session, and causal regime, and returns a joint probability with a conservative interval. A precision-versus-coverage threshold is chosen inside development folds. The decision engine abstains if the lower expected-net-edge bound is non-positive, calibration is stale, the interval is too wide, or the selected slice lacks evidence.

### 5. Execution and portfolio realism

Execution cost is computed from the decision-time quote and product schedule. The simulator supports spread crossing, fee tier, slippage, market impact, partial fill, queue uncertainty, latency, funding, borrow, and adverse intrabar ordering. Paper observations record predicted versus realized fill, slippage, latency, target/stop outcome, and missed-fill state. Those observations recalibrate execution assumptions but never rewrite historical decisions.

Portfolio validation includes gross and net exposure, asset and strategy concentration, correlation clusters, daily loss, drawdown, expected shortfall, and capacity. Promotion requires acceptable portfolio behavior in addition to isolated strategy metrics.

### 6. Validation policy

Named validation tiers replace ambiguous defaults:

- `exploratory`: development feedback only; never promotable.
- `research`: nested walk-forward, purge, embargo, trial accounting, and robustness evidence.
- `promotion`: at least 300 nominal trades, sufficient effective sample size, 99% bootstrap-positive and Deflated-Sharpe probabilities, PBO at most 10%, parameter stability at least 80%, maximum drawdown at most 10%, and a positive lower 95% net-edge confidence bound.
- `forward`: immutable shadow/paper evidence across multiple market regimes with execution-model error and drift gates.

Multiple rolling sealed holdouts replace reliance on one terminal split. Validation reports worst slices by asset, product, year, direction, session, volatility regime, and liquidity regime. Trial counts are global to the dataset/protocol/search family and cannot reset when code or configuration changes.

### 7. Governed self-improvement

Learning runs produce immutable challengers in a bounded grammar. Candidate search may use successive halving and deterministic Bayesian-style ranking, but every evaluated candidate consumes the global trial ledger. Rolling versus expanding training windows and recency decay are development-fold hyperparameters.

A challenger may become a research champion only after every offline gate passes. It then enters shadow mode. It cannot become live-qualified until a separately sealed forward cohort passes. Promotion is atomic, versioned, reversible, and logged. Rollback never restores expired evidence.

Drift monitors track feature distributions, prediction distributions, calibration residuals, realized cost, latency, and net edge. Adaptive-window mean-change and Bayesian-style run-length detectors produce warnings; confirmed material drift invalidates the readiness receipt and forces abstention.

### 8. Resource governance

Research workers default to `max(1, activeProcessorCount - 2)` and respect a configurable ceiling. At least two logical processors remain available for the operating system, UI, and live monitor. Thermal serious/critical, memory pressure, low disk space, sleep, or live heartbeat deterioration prevents new research work and checkpoints progress safely.

### 9. Native macOS evidence experience

Signals and live setups display:

- the exact provider, feed, venue, and product;
- probability definition (`target before stop after costs`);
- calibration method, sample and effective sample sizes;
- Brier score, calibration error, and conservative probability range;
- gross edge, expected cost, lower net-edge bound, and model age;
- current regime, drift state, quote latency, and data coverage;
- stop/target support and an explicit abstention reason.

The interface uses native SwiftUI containers, semantic colours, SF Symbols, Dynamic Type-compatible typography, keyboard navigation, VoiceOver labels, reduced-motion behavior, and system-standard disclosure. Confidence is never shown without its definition and sample context.

## Storage and compatibility

Database migrations are additive. New nullable market fields preserve old fixtures; promotable evidence requires the richer contract. App snapshots gain optional accuracy and drift fields so an older stored snapshot still decodes. Protocol/schema versions increase only when a consumer-visible contract changes, and Python/Swift fixture parity remains mandatory.

## Failure behavior

- Missing entitlement or credentials: exact provider is unavailable; no fallback masquerades as equivalent.
- Sequence gap or correction uncertainty: affected scope freezes and repairs before inference resumes.
- Insufficient calibration or effective sample: probability unavailable and signal abstains.
- Unsupported short mechanism: short signal is research-only and cannot alert as executable.
- Drift, stale evidence, or cost-model error: readiness receipt is invalidated.
- Worker, thermal, or resource failure: research checkpoints without affecting live monitoring.
- No candidate passes: the incumbent remains unchanged and the app reports that no reliable strategy was found.

## Testing

Python tests cover rich provider decoding, storage round trips, point-in-time barriers, adverse intrabar ordering, empirical risk levels, calibration metrics, selective thresholds, validation tiers, rolling holdouts, effective sample size, drift, global trial accounting, resource budgets, cohort resets, and fail-closed live decisions.

Swift tests cover backward-compatible snapshot decoding, evidence formatting, reliability presentation, accessibility labels, unknown enum handling, and abstention details. Full Python tests, Ruff formatting/lint, Swift tests, app build, deterministic live replay, snapshot parity, secret scan, engine bundle, and release verification are required before publication.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Rich Binance and Alpaca event payloads are normalized without discarding decision-relevant fields.
2. Historical and live stores retain provenance and sequence/latency evidence.
3. Barrier outcomes are causal, execution-aware, and safe under ambiguous intrabar ordering.
4. Live probabilities come from versioned out-of-fold calibration evidence with adequate effective sample size.
5. Static ATR targets are replaced by development-only empirical target/stop support or abstention.
6. Named validation tiers prevent exploratory evidence from being promoted.
7. Learning is champion/challenger, trial-accounted, drift-aware, and unable to self-deploy.
8. Research cannot starve the live monitor or UI.
9. The macOS app explains probability, uncertainty, costs, data identity, drift, and readiness in beginner-readable language.
10. Without entitled data and elapsed forward evidence, the system remains correctly live-locked.
